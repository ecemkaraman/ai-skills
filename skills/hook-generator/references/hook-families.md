# Hook families

Each family is a different psychological reason to keep watching or reading. Pick families
the content can actually support. Templates are starting shapes, not fill-in-the-blank
copy. Swap the brackets for something specific and true.

## 1. Recognition
The viewer thinks: "that's exactly my problem."
- `I keep [BEHAVIOUR], but I still [FRUSTRATION].`
- `Everyone tells me to [ADVICE], but nobody explains [MISSING PIECE].`
- `I don't need another [THING]. I need [ACTUAL NEED].`

## 2. Belief flip
Challenges a common assumption. The flip must reveal something, not just provoke.
- `You don't need [POPULAR THING] for [SPECIFIC TASK].`
- `Everyone is optimising for [X]. I'd optimise for [Y].`
- `The problem with [POPULAR IDEA] isn't [EXPECTED PROBLEM]. It's [REAL PROBLEM].`
- `Unpopular opinion: [DEFENSIBLE TAKE].`

## 3. Missing piece
Common advice is incomplete; here's the part that matters.
- `Everyone tells you to [ADVICE]. Nobody tells you [MISSING PART].`
- `[COMMON TIP] works, but only if [CONDITION].`

## 4. Partial reveal
Opens a specific gap. Never empty curiosity like "you won't believe what happened".
- `The interesting part of [THING] isn't [OBVIOUS PART].`
- `This looks like [X]. It's actually closer to [Y].`

## 5. Hidden mechanism
Explains why something behaves the way it does. Reaction: "oh, that's why."
- `Here's what actually happens when you [ACTION].`
- `If your [THING] keeps [BEHAVIOUR], I don't think [ASSUMED CAUSE] is the problem.`

## 6. Experiment
Only when the test is real.
- `Everyone says [CLAIM], so I tested it.`
- `I gave [SYSTEM] the same task and changed one thing.`
- `I compared [X] and [Y] on the same real task.`

## 7. Result first
Lead with the outcome, then show how. Measurable claims need evidence.
- `This one change fixed [SPECIFIC PROBLEM].`
- `I cut [PROCESS] from [X] to [Y].`

## 8. Build reveal
Only when there is a real build. Reaction: "show me."
- `I got tired of [FRICTION], so I built [THING].`
- `I built a [TOOL] that [SPECIFIC JOB].`

## 9. Diagnosis
Names a symptom and points at the real cause.
- `If [PROBLEM] keeps happening, I'd stop blaming [COMMON ASSUMPTION].`
- `Before you [COMMON FIX], check [THING].`

## 10. Utility / decision
A rule, check or choice the viewer can use today. A count must match the content.
- `If you only change one thing about [WORKFLOW], make it this.`
- `Here's my rule for deciding between [X] and [Y].`
- `If I were setting up [X] from scratch today, I'd start here.`

## 11. Discovery
A capability or detail most people haven't found.
- `[TOOL] has a [FEATURE] almost nobody uses, and it [SPECIFIC BENEFIT].`

## 12. Stakes
What goes wrong if you ignore this. No fear-mongering; the cost must be real.
- `This is the mistake that cost me [REAL COST].`
- `If you [ACTION] without [CHECK], this is what happens.`

## 13. Unexpected connection
Links the topic to an idea from somewhere else that makes it click.
- `[TOPIC] makes a lot more sense once you think of it like [ANALOGY].`

## 14. Borrowed method
Applies a proven method from another field.
- `[PROFESSION] have a trick for [PROBLEM], and it works surprisingly well for [YOUR TOPIC].`

## Modifier: story promise
Adds a narrative pull to any family. Not a family on its own.
- `The first time I tried [X], [UNEXPECTED THING] happened.`
